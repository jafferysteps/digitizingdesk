Terms & Machine Guarantee

We believe in simple, clear business terms written the way decorators talk. No microscopic legalese or deceptive clauses.

1. The Machine Error Guarantee (Free Fixes)

If any file delivered by DigitizingDesk fails on your machine, breaks thread repeatedly, causes fabric puckering, or exhibits misregistration due to our digitizing error, we will fix and adjust it completely free of charge. Tell us the symptom or send a phone photo of the sew-out, and our team will adjust pull comp, density, or underlay and return the file within 2 to 4 hours.

2. Two Free Revision Rounds

Every order includes 2 free revision rounds within 7 calendar days of delivery for minor sizing adjustments, stitch density tweaks, or format conversions. Substantial changes to the original artwork or requests to digitize a completely new logo after approval are billed as new work.

3. Refund Policy

We stand behind every file we build. If we are unable to make an embroidery design run clean on your machine after two consecutive fix attempts, we will immediately refund 100% of the price of that design.

4. 100% Client Artwork Ownership

You and your customers own all rights, title, and intellectual property in the artwork and digitized production files we produce for you. DigitizingDesk will never sell, license, reuse, or share your designs or client logos with third parties.

5. Turnaround Commitments

Standard orders are completed within 12 business hours. Rush orders are completed within 2 to 4 business hours (+50% rush surcharge applies). Turnaround clock begins once all artwork details, sizes, and fabrics are clarified.

6. Invoicing and Payment Terms

First-time customers pay upon file delivery after inspecting the preview. After 3 completed orders, shops in good standing can transition to monthly invoicing (net-15 or net-30) upon request.

DigitizingDesk is a production-art partner for US decoration shops. We turn customer artwork into files that run clean on the machine.
