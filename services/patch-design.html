FIVE PATCH CATEGORIES

Whatever your client wants, we prepare the files.

From traditional merrowed scout badges to multi-tier 3D PVC rubber patches for tactical gear.

EMBROIDERED PATCHES

Classic Twill & Stitch

Includes digitized machine stitch file (.DST), twill background selection, and perimeter stitch with merrowed or laser-cut edge.

PVC & RUBBER

2D & 3D Mold Art

Layered vector files with recessed/embossed step levels, needle stitching groove channel, and Pantone color codes for steel mold cutting.

LEATHER & LEATHERETTE

Debossed & Laser Engraved

Clean vector files pre-sized for brass debossing stamps or CO2 laser engraving with burn-darkening parameters.

CHENILLE & FELT

Varsity & Collegiate

Loop-pile chain stitch pathing combined with single or double felt backboard cutlines for traditional letterman jackets.

WOVEN PATCHES

Micro-Detail & Small Text

Fine thread weaving setup for intricate logos where text is too small (under 4mm) to stitch cleanly as standard embroidery.
