PLACEMENTS & FABRICS

Every placement runs differently. We program for each one.

A left chest digitizing file will fail if run on a curved cap frame. We build files specifically for the garment and hoop you are running.

LEFT CHEST & POLOS

Pique, Performance & Tees

Programmed with cross-hatch underlay to stop delicate piques from sinking. Extra pull compensation prevents letters from distorting on stretchy athletic poly.

CAPS & HATS

Structured & Unstructured

Pathed bottom-up, center-out to push fabric away from the needle and prevent crown bunching or center-seam birdnesting on 6-panel caps.

JACKET BACKS

Large Multi-Layer Designs

Balanced tatami fill angles and segmented underlays prevent the garment from puckering or becoming a stiff, heavy cardboard patch on hoodies and jackets.

3D FOAM PUFF

Raised Dimensional Lettering

Dual capping stitches close column ends so foam cannot poke through, with automated needle perforations for clean, easy foam tear-away.

APPLIQUE

Twill & Specialty Fabrics

Three distinct machine steps: running placement guide stitch, material tackdown, and heavy satin framing edge with laser alignment markers.

BEANIES & FLEECE

High-Pile Knit Fabrics

Dense knockdown / lattice base stitching matts down loose yarn fibers so satin text floats clean on top without getting swallowed by the knit.
