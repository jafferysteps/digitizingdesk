## 📌 Overview
DigitizingDesk is a production-art partner for US embroidery, screen print, patch, and DTF decoration shops. We turn customer artwork into stitch-ready and press-ready files fast, with sew-out proofs and free fixes if anything misbehaves on the machine.

### Core Brand Values
- **Steady:** Calm, dependable, never hyped.
- **Shop-floor fluent:** We speak pull comp, underlay, mesh count and gang sheets without explaining them.
- **Precise:** Specific numbers, specific file types, 12-hour standard turnaround.
- **Warm:** Friendly like a good coworker, not a corporate vendor.

## 🗂️ Site Architecture
- **Home (`index.html`):** Hero, trust strip, 4-step workflow, 5-service catalog, interactive Before/After image comparison slider, live Quick Pricing Estimator, shop proof gallery, FAQ accordion, closing CTA.
- **Pricing & Rate Card (`pricing.html`):** Full rate cards for all 5 services, volume discount tiers, Prepaid Desk Balance bonus tiers (10% to 20% bonus credit), and launch offer.
- **Quote Machine (`get-quote.html`):** High-converting intake form with drag & drop multi-file upload (up to 50MB), placement, finished size, garment/fabric specs, and 30-minute quote response feedback.
- **Work & Proofs (`work.html`):** Category-filterable gallery of real machine sew-outs, vector before-and-after redraws, color separations, and patch spec sheets.
- **About (`about.html`):** The desk story, 4 brand pillars, 9-point pre-flight quality checklist, and Digital Minds Agency (DMA) backing.
- **Decorator FAQ (`faq.html`):** Direct answers covering machine compatibility (.DST/.PES), turnarounds, thread breaks, artwork ownership, and free fixes.
- **Services:**
  - `services/embroidery-digitizing.html` - Left chest, caps, jackets, 3D puff, applique
  - `services/vector-artwork.html` - Raster-to-vector cleanup, outlined fonts, welded paths
  - `services/color-separations.html` - Spot, CMYK, simulated process, choked underbase
  - `services/patch-design.html` - Embroidered, PVC, leather, woven, chenille patches
  - `services/dtf-gang-sheets.html` - 22" wide nested layouts, gradient halftoning
- **Legal & Policies:**
  - `terms.html` - Plain-English machine guarantee and free fix policy
  - `privacy.html` - 100% client artwork ownership and strict confidentiality
