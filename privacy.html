DATA SECURITY & ART PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

At DigitizingDesk, we take decorator confidentiality seriously. You trust us with proprietary client logos and rush orders; here is how we protect your information.

1. Artwork Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality

Every file, logo, trademark, sketch, and customer mockup submitted to DigitizingDesk is treated as strictly proprietary and confidential. We never display client work publicly on our website or social media without written authorization from the decorator.

2. White-Label Protection

We respect the decorator-client relationship. If you outsource artwork to us, we will never contact your end-clients, market to them, or embed our branding in production sheets intended for your customers.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the business contact details required to process your quote and deliver files: your name, shop name, business email, phone number (optional), and production specifications. We do not sell or trade your email address to advertisers.

4. Secure File Storage

All artwork uploaded through our forms is stored on secure, encrypted cloud infrastructure. Files are preserved for 12 months so you can request re-downloads or format changes anytime without having to re-upload.

5. Parent Company Disclosure

DigitizingDesk is a commercial enterprise owned and operated under Digital Minds Agency (DMA). For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us directly at orders@digitizingdesk.com.
