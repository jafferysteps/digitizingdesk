COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you send your art.

Direct shop-floor answers. No corporate doublespeak.

For embroidery: .DST and .PES are delivered as standard with every job. We also provide .EMB, .EXP, .JEF, .XXX, and .VP3 upon request at no extra charge. Every delivery includes an easy-to-read PDF production sheet (size, stitch count, thread color sequence) and a high-res PNG stitch preview.

For vectors: .AI, .EPS, .SVG, and print-ready PDF with outlined fonts and named spot colors.

Yes. Tajima .DST is the universal industry standard and runs on Tajima, Barudan, Melco, Ricoma, Brother, SWF, Happy, ZSK, and all commercial multi-head machines. When you send artwork, tell us your garment or fabric type (e.g. pique polo, structured 6-panel cap, hoodie, lightweight poly performance), and our digitizers calibrate the pull compensation, stitch density, and underlay specifically for that material so the file runs clean without puckering or thread breaks.

Yes. Over 60% of what decorators receive from customers is a blurry phone photo, a screenshot, or a web JPG. Our team redraws the artwork cleanly first to establish crisp vector anchors, ensuring every letter and satin border stitches straight and sharp.

Standard turnaround is within 12 business hours (most jobs delivered in 8 to 10 hours). If you have a customer waiting at the counter or an urgent press run, select our Rush option (+50%) for guaranteed delivery within 2 to 4 hours.

We fix it free. If you encounter thread breaks, birdnesting, puckering, or registration shifts due to our digitizing error, tell us what happened (or send a quick phone photo of the sew-out). We adjust pull compensation, density, or underlay and return the fixed file within 2 to 4 hours at zero charge. If we cannot make it run clean after two fix attempts, we refund that file in full.

You and your customer do. Your artwork stays strictly confidential. We never reuse, resell, or share your designs. White-label files are delivered completely unbranded so you can pass them directly to your clients.
